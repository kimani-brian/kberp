# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in KB ERP to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the code and kind about the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming a motive.
- Give and accept technical criticism that is specific and actionable.
- Respect that maintainers decide scope and timing.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory comments, sexualized attention,
personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing private information
without consent are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any
other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Do not open a public issue about it. Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close
threads, or temporarily or permanently ban a participant.
